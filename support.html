Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my Strava account?

On first launch, tap “Connect with Strava” and allow access to your activities. Keep the box that lets the app read all your activities checked: without it, the app cannot show your workouts.

How do I disconnect from Strava?

In the app: Settings tab → “Disconnect”. Access is revoked at Strava and the data stored on your phone is erased. You can also revoke access from Strava: Settings → My Apps.

My sets are not recognized

Trente30 recognizes sets from your watch laps: press the lap button at each change of effort, or use a planned workout. You can also write the structure in your activity title on Strava, for example “8x1'/1'”, “10x400 r200” or “3x(600/500/400/300/200)”.

How do I share to my Instagram story?

In the editor, tap the share button at the top right, then choose Instagram, or “Save Image” to add it to your photo library and post it later. A visual with a transparent background is saved as a transparent PNG: add it to your story from the sticker picker or by pasting it.

How do I restore my purchases?

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, go to Settings → Purchases → “Restore purchases” (or from the premium pack page), with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why are the start and end of my route cut off?

So your home isn’t revealed, the first and last 500 meters of the route are hidden by default. In Settings → Privacy, you can choose 200 m, 500 m, 1 km, or turn hiding off.
